Who it’s for, and the problem

Who it’s for
  • Small federal and state contractors bidding on RFPs and RFQs
  • Small-business and set-aside bidders, such as SDVOSB firms
  • Owner-run teams without a dedicated proposal manager
The problem

Every “shall” has to be answered, amendments quietly change the requirements, and deadlines for questions and quotes are easy to miss. One overlooked instruction can sink an otherwise strong quote.

What you get

Solicitation breakdown

We read the RFP or RFQ and every amendment, then pull out scope, set-aside, evaluation factors and instructions.

Compliance matrix

Each requirement mapped to the section of your response that answers it, delivered as PDF and Excel.

Bid/no-bid summary

A short brief with the red flags called out, so you can decide quickly whether the bid is worth your time.

Proposal outline

An outline that follows the submission instructions and the order of the evaluation criteria.

First-draft technical response

Draft narrative written to the evaluation criteria, ready for you to edit and add detail.

Dates and deadlines

Question deadlines, amendment dates and due dates, with time zones, in one place.
