Who it’s for, and the problem

Who it’s for

Questions

Is virtual staging allowed on the MLS?

Rules differ by MLS and brokerage. Label staged images as virtually staged and keep the original photos available. Check with your MLS if you are unsure.

Do you offer 3D tours?

Not full walkthrough tours. We can add a depth-based 3D parallax zoom, like the sample above, to a listing reel. It is a perspective video made from a still photo, not a 3D scan.

What do you need from me?

The listing link or the original photos. Wide, well-lit shots of empty rooms give the best results.

Can a brokerage get a recurring arrangement?

Yes. A monthly retainer is available. Email us how many listings you handle and we will quote it.
