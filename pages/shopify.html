Checkout & Email Retention Teardowns

Who it’s for, and the problem

Who it’s for
  • Shopify and direct-to-consumer brands that already collect emails
  • Founders who know their emails and product pages could work harder but lack the time
The problem

Welcome emails read like templates, abandoned-cart reminders are easy to ignore, and product pages leave questions unanswered at the moment a shopper decides.

What you get

Welcome email audit

A review of your welcome emails: timing, offer, tone and what the first message asks the reader to do.

Abandoned-cart audit

A review of your cart reminders and how they bring the shopper back to checkout.

Product page review

Notes on headlines, descriptions, proof and calls to action on your key pages.

Paste-ready rewritten copy

New subject lines and email copy formatted so you can drop it into your email platform.

Prioritized fix list

The changes most likely to matter first, so you know where to start.

After setup, ongoing management of your flows if you want it.
